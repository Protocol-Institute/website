  • C3PO Live · Beta In-House

    A RAG-based conversational research assistant trained on the Protocol Institute's full corpus — 12,000+ vectors spanning papers, essays, talks, Discord discussions, SIG meetings, and Protocolized magazine content. C3PO lets researchers and practitioners query, synthesize, and explore the library through natural language, with citations. Powered by Voyage AI embeddings, Pinecone, and Claude Sonnet. Also available as an MCP server for use with Claude Code and Claude Desktop.
