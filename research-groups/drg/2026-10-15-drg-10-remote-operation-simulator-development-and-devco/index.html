DRG#10: Remote Operation, Simulator Development, and Devcon Mumbai Game Demo

Real-world robotics applications as accelerators for research directionMaking distributed robotics accessible to sponsors and investors through interactive demosYakrobot simulator development and implementationYoink game adaptation for real robot control at Devcon MumbaiOnchain flag capture mechanics for remote robot operation

Participants: sn37, anurajenp

The DRG group discussed the importance of real-world applications and events in shaping their robotics research direction. A key strategic point emerged around making their work legible to potential sponsors and investors—while interactive games can serve as engaging entry points, there must be a clear narrative linking the fun experience to concrete future applications with real economic impact. The group is progressing on simulator development, with anurajenp sharing the yakrobot-simulator repository. For Devcon Mumbai, the team is planning an innovative demonstration: adapting the yoink game (previously played on Farcaster) to control actual robots, where participants would capture an onchain flag to operate the robot, with results tracked on a leaderboard. This demo exemplifies their strategy of using interactive experiences to make distributed robotics tangible and exciting to a broad audience.

  • Real-world applications and events expose problems that wouldn't surface in theoretical work and help ground research direction more quickly and effectively.
  • Interactive games and demos are valuable evangelizing mechanisms to create the 'aha moment' for potential sponsors, but require explicit framing connecting the fun experience to tangible future economic applications.
  • The group plans to adapt the Farcaster yoink game for real robot control at Devcon Mumbai, using onchain flag capture as the mechanic to operate robots and tracking performance on a leaderboard.
