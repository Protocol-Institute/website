Commerce in the Thicket

Toward a new culture of marketing in the AI era. A briefing for the Protocol Institute AI-Native Marketing Hackathon, Q1 2027 (date to be announced).

How to develop and demonstrate strategies and tactics for successful, healthy commerce in AI-native media, using the Blygger protocol as a testbed. It covers a doctrine of commercially clean fighting, the thicket as a commercial habitat, the agentic attention economy, the economics of capture resistance, and a red-team view of adversarial commerce, with eight open challenges for the hackathon.

This is an evolving document; each version is kept at its own address.
