Memory Research Group Session: Cache Memories Discussion

Participants: keikreutler, azlewis, chenoe_hart, waveywaves.

The Memory Research Group convened on October 30, 2025, to discuss cache memories with approximately 6 participants. The session was structured around collaborative note-taking in a shared Google Doc. The facilitator, keikreutler, posed three guiding questions designed to orient the group's analysis: examining metaphors of attention, locality, and hierarchy; identifying human decision-making embedded in machine design; and exploring how computational trade-offs (speed, size, coherence) mirror constraints in other complex systems. The meeting was in early stages when the transcript ends, with participants beginning to engage with the core discussion topics.

  • The group established three guiding questions to frame analysis of a paper on cache memories, focusing on metaphorical language and design philosophy.
  • There is interest in identifying where human choices manifest in technical design decisions, particularly in memory architecture.
  • Trade-offs between performance metrics (speed, size, coherence) in computing systems may parallel similar constraints in biological, social, and ecological systems.
