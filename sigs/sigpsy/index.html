SIGPSY — Special Interest Group in Psychohistory

Studying long-range historical modeling and prediction — drawing on quantitative history, complexity science, and protocol theory to develop frameworks for understanding civilizational-scale dynamics. The group maintains worldmachines.org, a collaborative platform for psychohistorical modeling.
