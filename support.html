Support Us

Protocol Institute is a new kind of think tank, a context tank, devoted to furthering the study and application of protocols, and furthering protocolization of the world.

We are actively looking for support from both high-net-worth individual donors and suitable funding and partner organizations/agencies.

If you're interested in supporting our mission, please contact the Managing Director, Timber Stinson-Schroff at timber@protocol-institute.org, or the Director of Research, Venkatesh Rao at venkat@protocol-institute.org.

We will shortly have more details on this page about what kinds of programs we have, and the various specific ways you can support us.
