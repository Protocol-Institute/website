Summer of Protocols

NoticeWe are currently migrating the old Summer of Protocols content to our new domains.

Summer of Protocols was the research program, funded by the Ethereum Foundation, that the Protocol Institute grew out of. It brought together researchers from many disciplines to investigate the deep structure of protocols as a category.

Where the content is going

  • Essays, papers, tools, talks and PDFs are published on our media hub, Protocolized.
  • Institutional pages (about, programs, events, people) are here on protocol-institute.org. Start with About and Programs.
  • Fiction now lives at Monstrous Times.
