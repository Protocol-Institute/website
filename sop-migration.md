Content and pages from summerofprotocols.com that belong on protocol-institute.org once
the domain is redirected. Tracked here so nothing gets lost in the transition.

See `protocolized-website/sop-migration-items.md` for the parallel list of items that
belong on protocolized.io.

## 1. SoP Program History — About page

**What:** A detailed program narrative covering SoP23, SoP24, and SoP25: research tracks,
participant counts, funding ($2.5M from Ethereum Foundation), output stats (70+ alumni,
100+ outputs, 358-page proto-textbook, 1,800+ subscribers), and conference/event history
(Seattle, Singapore, Healdsburg, Chiang Mai, Devconnect Buenos Aires).

**Where:** Expand `about/index.html` with a "Program History" or "Origins" section.
The current About page mentions SoP's origins in one paragraph; the full history is
institutional memory that must be preserved before the redirect.

**Source:** summerofprotocols.com/about
