Protocol Institute Network

Draft — content coming soon

The Protocol Institute Network is a network of organizations engaged in research, application, education, or scene-making initiatives related to protocols and protocolization. University-based research groups and labs, publications, hobbyist groups, and private corporations involved in protocol technologies or protocolized operations are eligible to join.

The Protocol Institute curates this network to foster collaboration among members, as well as develop and maintain shared public commons resources.

To be added to this page, please contact Timber Stinson-Schroff or Venkatesh Rao.
