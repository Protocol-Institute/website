Protocol Symposium 2026

The Protocol Institute is convening the Protocol Symposium 2026 in September 2026. The symposium brings together researchers, practitioners, and institutions working across the challenges and opportunities of protocolization.

The theme of the 2026 symposium is New Nature. Full details on the theme are in this Protocolized essay.

Submissions Update

Submissions have closed for the main track. If you've submitted a proposal you'll hear from us soon. We will have a shortlist stage and an experimental PI member voting process to help shape the agenda. If you've arranged with us to curate a special session, we will be in touch for additional details shortly.
