Symposium — September 21–25, 2026

Protocol Symposium 2026

The Protocol Institute is convening the Protocol Symposium 2026 in September 2026. The symposium brings together researchers, practitioners, and institutions working across the challenges and opportunities of protocolization. We invite submissions for talks and workshops. Submission deadline is Sunday, June 14.

The theme of the 2026 symposium is New Nature. Full details on the theme are in this Protocolized essay. If you are looking for ideas for submissions, the Protocol Institute Challenges page is a good place to start.

Call for Submissions

We invite submissions of two kinds:

Short presentations (25 minutes + 5 minutes Q&A): Completed or in-progress work on any aspect of protocol research — theoretical, applied, historical, or speculative.

Workshop proposals: A facilitated session on a specific protocol-related theme, challenge, or practice. Workshops run as 4 Zoom sessions over 2 days (September 21–22).

Special Sessions: If you're interested in hosting a special session (4 talks) please contact Venkatesh Rao.

Submission deadline: Sunday, June 14
