Draft Program

We recommend voting via a desktop browser.

Instead of peer review, we're experimenting with quadratically weighted approval voting. All shortlisted proposals that attract interest above a threshold will be accepted (possibly all of them). You have 55 votes to distribute as you like among the 55 proposals. Voting is open to all PI members for 3 days: Wednesday June 17 to Saturday June 20. You can freely edit your votes until then. Scroll down the "All" tab applying votes to proposals. You can also filter by special sessions on the "Talks" tab and find the workshops on the Workshops tab.

You can also add a comment to any proposal for the benefit of the proposer.

Indicated interest level is not meant as a measure of either quality or importance, and will primarily be used to guide scheduling and gauge broad patterns of interest.

Each member receives a vote budget of 55 votes. Votes per proposal are quadratically weighted: n votes → √n weight. Team members vote at 3×, community leads at 2×, regular members at 1×.

Log in as a PI member to view proposals and participate in voting.
