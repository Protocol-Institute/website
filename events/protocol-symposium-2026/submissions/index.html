Instead of regular peer review, we're experimenting with quadratically weighted approval voting, and all shortlisted proposals that attract interest above a threshold will be accepted. Interest level is not meant as a measure of either quality or importance, and will primarily be used to guide scheduling and gauge broad patterns of interest.
