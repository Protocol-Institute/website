Conference Event — November 22, 2025 — Buenos Aires

Bridge Atlas at Devconnect 2025

Bridge Atlas was a day-long gathering convened at Devconnect Buenos Aires on November 22, 2025. Organized by the Summer of Protocols team, the event brought together researchers, practitioners, and community builders for a structured program of talks and small-group exercises exploring how protocols can serve as bridges — across disciplines, communities, and institutions.

Drawing on three years of accumulated protocol research, creative projects, and educational initiatives, the program was designed to surface cross-domain connections: where protocol frameworks developed in one field illuminate problems in another, and where the act of bridge-building itself becomes a protocol design question. Small-group sessions gave participants structured space to develop these connections collaboratively.

Bridge Atlas was the culminating public event of the Summer of Protocols era, held alongside Devconnect's broader gathering of the Ethereum research and development community.
