Protocol Foundations Workshop 2025

The Protocol Foundations Workshop was a focused three-day research sprint held September 12–14, 2025, as the opening component of Protocol Symposium 2025. Up to thirty researchers and practitioners participated across multiple Zoom sessions, working collaboratively toward a shared goal: developing a formal, mathematizable theory of protocols.

Participants were researchers and practitioners selected by the program team — combining background in formal methods, organizational theory, computer science, and protocol studies. The workshop aimed to produce a first-draft whitepaper and a structured research program as concrete outputs, moving protocol theory from a primarily descriptive field toward one amenable to formal analysis and computation.

The Foundations Workshop followed immediately into the Protocol School 2025, which ran September 15–19 as the educational component of the same symposium week.
