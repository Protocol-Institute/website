Protocol School 2025

The inaugural Protocol School ran September 15–19, 2025 as the educational centerpiece of Protocol Symposium 2025, organized around the theme Accelerating Order. Twelve 90-minute learning modules were developed and delivered by eleven university teaching fellows over five days, covering protocol theory and practice at a college level.

The program was designed for students, researchers, and professionals with or without a technical background. Certification was available for participants who met completion requirements. Hackathon and coworking sessions ran in parallel throughout the week, providing space for applied work and informal collaboration alongside the structured curriculum.

Protocol School is designed as a biennial initiative. The next edition is planned for 2027. For the full curriculum, teaching fellows roster, and program details, see the Protocol School program page.
