Member Login / Register

Enter your email address to continue. Existing members will be directed to their profile to view or update it. New applicants can complete a brief form to request membership.

Membership is open to individuals who have participated in a qualifying Protocol Institute event (Summer of Protocols grant cohort, Protocol School, a PI symposium event), are an active participant in a SIG, have received a Protocol Kit, or have contributed writing to Protocolized. Once approved, your details will appear on the Members page.

If we have your email from prior event participation, your application will be approved immediately. If we don't have your email, or you use a different one, it may take a few days to a week for manual approval. Approval decision emails will be sent from noreply@protocol-institute.org. If you do not see an email within a week, check your spam folder, and mark it not-spam if it landed there. If you do not see it at all, please post a bug report in the Discord #bugs-and-tests channel.
