The Protocol Institute is pleased to announce the Protocol Symposium 2026, to be held in September 2026 (exact dates to be confirmed). The symposium brings together researchers, practitioners, and institutions working across the many challenges and opportunities of protocolization.

We invite submissions of two kinds:

**Short presentations** (20 minutes): An abstract of 200–400 words describing completed or in-progress work on any aspect of protocol research — theoretical, applied, historical, or speculative.

**Workshop proposals**: A proposal of 400–600 words describing a facilitated session on a specific protocol-related theme, challenge, or practice. Workshops may run 60–90 minutes.

To submit, please use [this form](GOOGLE_FORM_URL). Submission deadline and full program details to be announced.
