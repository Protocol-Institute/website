Protocol School

Protocol School is a biennial intensive educational program in protocol theory and practice. It is held as part of the Protocol Symposium and developed by a rotating cohort of university teaching fellows. The next edition is planned for 2027.

2025 Edition

September 15–19, 2025 — Online

The inaugural Protocol School ran over five days as part of the Protocol Symposium 2025, organized around the theme Accelerating Order. Twelve 90-minute learning modules were developed and delivered by 11 university teaching fellows drawn from the Summer of Protocols research community.

Format

Twelve modules over five days, covering protocol theory and practice at a college level. Suitable for students, researchers, and professionals; some technology background was recommended but not required. Certification was available for participants who met completion requirements. Hackathon and coworking sessions ran in parallel for hands-on application.

Teaching Fellows

The 2025 cohort comprised 11 university-affiliated teaching fellows who developed original curriculum for the program. A full list will be added here as records are gathered.
